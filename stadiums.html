The homes of Indian cricket

Iconic international stadiums in India.

Explore famous international cricket grounds across India, with a short introduction, a visual glimpse and one or more moments that helped make each venue special.

Grounds & memories

Stadiums that made history

This selection covers major active international venues and some of India's best-known cricket grounds. Events focus on memorable Test, ODI and T20I moments rather than every match played at each stadium.

Ahmedabad · Gujarat

Narendra Modi Stadium

The world's largest cricket stadium, with a capacity of around 132,000. It is one of India's main venues for major internationals and global tournament matches.

Iconic event — 2023 ODI World Cup Final Australia defeated India in the final at Ahmedabad on 19 November 2023. The venue also hosted a famous 1987 milestone when Sunil Gavaskar became the first batter to pass 10,000 Test runs.
Kolkata · West Bengal

Eden Gardens

Established in 1864, Eden Gardens is one of world cricket's most historic and atmospheric venues, beside Kolkata's Hooghly River.

Iconic events — 1987 World Cup Final & 2001 Test It became the first stadium outside Lord's to host a men's World Cup final in 1987. In 2001, VVS Laxman and Rahul Dravid helped India complete a famous comeback win over Australia.
Mumbai · Maharashtra

Wankhede Stadium

Built in 1974, Wankhede is one of India's most recognisable cricket venues and a major international ground in Mumbai.

Iconic event — 2011 World Cup Final India defeated Sri Lanka at Wankhede on 2 April 2011 to win the World Cup on home soil. The ground also hosted Sachin Tendulkar's final international appearance.
Chennai · Tamil Nadu

M. A. Chidambaram Stadium

Better known as Chepauk, this historic ground dates to 1916 and has hosted international cricket since 1934.

Iconic events — 1952 & 1986 India recorded its first Test victory over England here in 1952. In 1986, Chepauk hosted the famous tied Test between India and Australia.
Delhi · India

Arun Jaitley Stadium

One of India's oldest international cricket venues, the ground sits beside the historic Feroz Shah Kotla fort in New Delhi.

Iconic event — Tendulkar's 35th Test century In 2005, Sachin Tendulkar scored his 35th Test century against Sri Lanka at the venue, moving past Sunil Gavaskar's then record for Test centuries.
Bengaluru · Karnataka

M. Chinnaswamy Stadium

A compact, high-scoring international venue in the centre of Bengaluru and a regular home for India's international matches.

Iconic event — 2011 World Cup classic Ireland's Kevin O'Brien smashed a 50-ball century against England during the 2011 World Cup, then the fastest century in men's ODI World Cup history.
Hyderabad · Telangana

Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium

Located in Uppal, Hyderabad's major international venue has become an important home ground for the city and the national cricket calendar.

Iconic event — 2017 IPL Final The stadium hosted the 2017 IPL final, adding a major tournament showpiece to its international and domestic cricket history.
Dharamshala · Himachal Pradesh

HPCA Stadium

Set against the Himalayan landscape, Dharamshala is one of India's most scenic international cricket grounds and a regular international venue since 2013.

Iconic event — 2023 World Cup The venue became a major stage during the 2023 ODI World Cup, hosting several group matches with the mountains providing a distinctive backdrop.
Lucknow · Uttar Pradesh

BRSABV Ekana Cricket Stadium

Opened in 2017, Ekana is a modern international venue and the home ground of Uttar Pradesh cricket and the Lucknow Super Giants.

Iconic event — first World Cup match in 2023 The stadium hosted its first men's ODI World Cup fixture during the 2023 tournament, marking a new chapter in Lucknow's international cricket history.
Mohali · Punjab

PCA IS Bindra Stadium

Mohali has been a major northern Indian international venue, known for big matches and its lively atmosphere.

Iconic event — 2011 World Cup Semi-final India and Pakistan met in a hugely watched World Cup semi-final at Mohali, with India winning by 29 runs to reach the final.
Rajkot · Gujarat

Niranjan Shah Stadium

Rajkot's international venue has hosted Tests, ODIs and T20Is and is an important cricket ground in Saurashtra.

Iconic event — India's early international milestones Rajkot has hosted memorable India home internationals across formats and became a regular part of the country's international venue network.
