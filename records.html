Numbers that made history

Unique records of Indian cricketers.

From Sachin Tendulkar's century milestone to Ashwin's rapid route to 300 Test wickets, these records show the extraordinary achievements of India's cricket legends.

Indian cricket legends

Records worth remembering

A selection of distinctive international records and milestones associated with Indian players. Figures are based on official ICC records and profiles available in 2026.

Sachin Tendulkar

100 international centuries

100 × 100s

Tendulkar became the first player to score 100 international centuries: 51 in Tests and 49 in ODIs. He also finished with 34,357 international runs.

Rohit Sharma

Highest ODI score

264 runs

Rohit holds the highest individual score in men's ODIs. He is also the only batter to have three ODI double-centuries.

Virat Kohli

Fastest to 15,000 ODI runs

315 innings

In September 2026, Kohli became the fastest batter to reach 15,000 men's ODI runs, becoming only the second player to the landmark after Tendulkar.

MS Dhoni

Most ODI stumpings

123 stumpings

Dhoni holds the men's ODI record for wicketkeeper stumpings. ICC also lists his unbeaten 183 as the highest ODI score by a wicketkeeper.

Ravichandran Ashwin

Fastest to 300 Test wickets

54 Tests

Ashwin reached 300 Test wickets in his 54th match, becoming the fastest bowler in Test history to reach the landmark at the time.

Anil Kumble

India's leading Test wicket-taker

619 wickets

Kumble finished his Test career with 619 wickets, the most by an Indian bowler and one of the highest totals in Test history.

Kapil Dev

434 Test wickets

434 wickets

Kapil Dev took 434 Test wickets, a world record at the time. He was also the captain of India's historic 1983 World Cup-winning side.

Record note: Cricket records can change as active players continue their careers. This page highlights notable records verified from ICC material, with the 2026 Virat Kohli ODI milestone included.
