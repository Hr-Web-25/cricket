From a Test debut to a global powerhouse

India became a Test nation in 1932. The game grew through domestic cricket, iconic players and landmark victories, including the 1983 and 2011 ODI World Cups.

In 2026, India retained the Men's T20 World Cup on home soil, becoming the first side to defend the title.
